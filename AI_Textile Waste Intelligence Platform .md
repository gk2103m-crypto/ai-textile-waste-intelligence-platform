## Textile Waste Intelligence Platform

## 1. Title: Textile Waste Intelligence Platform

## 2. Objective

Build an AI-powered Textile Waste Intelligence Platform that analyzes textile waste using computer vision, material classification, and sustainability intelligence to identify fabric types, estimate recyclability, recommend recycling or reuse strategies, and optimize textile waste management.

The platform leverages image analysis, material recognition, waste classification, circular economy analytics, and AI-driven recommendation systems to help manufacturers, recycling facilities, fashion brands, retailers, and sustainability organizations reduce textile waste and improve resource recovery.

The solution supports sustainable fashion initiatives by providing intelligent waste sorting, recycling recommendations, environmental impact analysis, and circular economy insights.

## Outcomes

- Developed and deployed an AI-powered textile waste intelligence platform.

- Implemented secure authentication and role-based access control.

- Built textile image recognition and material classification systems.

- Developed waste categorization and recyclability prediction models.

- Implemented recycling and reuse recommendation engines.

- Built sustainability scoring and environmental impact analytics.

- Developed dashboards for manufacturers, recyclers, and sustainability teams.

- Deployed the platform using Docker and cloud deployment platforms such as AWS or Azure.
